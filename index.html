Strategy
  • Fill your arms. Three balls thrown together pay +40, and a PERFECT release doubles every ball.
  • Chase gold. A gold ball is 75, a green 30, a red only 10. Sprint for the glowing one.
  • Get clear first. He steals from your arms up close. Wait for the ring to turn green, then wind up.
  • Throw from far out. Bank from beyond the fire pit for a long-ball bonus on every ball.
  • Grab the bone. He drops everything to chew it — that's your free volley.
  • Save one for last call. The final 15 seconds bank double.
